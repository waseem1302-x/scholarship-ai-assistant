# Phase 9 beta notice pack

Status: draft for product-owner and legal approval. Versions below match the
server settings and acceptance record. Do not enable beta invitations until
approved copy, contacts, support hours, and the intended participant geography
are confirmed.

## Terms — `phase9.beta-terms.v1`

This is an invite-only product beta for scholarship discovery and application
support. It provides decision support, not a promise of scholarship funding,
eligibility, admission, visa approval, or any other outcome. Participants must
use accurate account information, keep access credentials and invitation codes
private, and comply with the Community policy when that optional feature is
enabled. Access may be paused or ended to protect participants, data quality,
or system safety. Participants can contact the published support owner about
account access, export, deletion, or product concerns.

Registration reserves an invitation, but the participant joins the beta only
after verifying the invited email address. Before then they cannot create or
change private profile, application, assistant, Document Lab, or Community
content. They can still request verification, recover their account, and use
their export/delete rights before verification.

## Privacy notice — `phase9.beta-privacy.v1`

The product processes account email, authentication/session data, profile
details, application-workspace data, assistant history when enabled, optional
Document Lab data, and optional Community data for the features a participant
uses. Each feature retains its separate consent and privacy boundaries. The
product does not use application notes, private documents, or Community text as
assistant evidence. Participants can use the account export and closure tools;
some minimal security/audit metadata and encrypted backups may persist only for
their documented retention periods. The final published notice must identify
the approved hosting, email, monitoring, storage, and AI processors, relevant
region, retention schedule, and incident contact.
